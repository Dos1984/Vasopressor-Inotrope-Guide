General principle
Reassess after every single bolus. Stop and escalate — start/optimise a vasopressor, get senior input — if there's no response after 2 boluses, or if you see signs of fluid overload.
Sources: NICE CG174 (IV fluid therapy in adults); NICE NG51/NG253 (suspected sepsis, 2025); Surviving Sepsis Campaign guidelines; REFRESH trial (restrictive vs. liberal fluid strategy). Educational summary — always follow local protocols.
